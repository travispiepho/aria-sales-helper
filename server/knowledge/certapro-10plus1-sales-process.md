## THE 10+1 SALES PROCESS — COMPLETE STAGES

---

### STAGE 1 — THE SETUP CALL (pre-visit)
**Goal:** Qualify, confirm, build rapport before arriving

**Must cover:**
- Greeting
- Appointment confirmation
- Source (how did they hear about us?)
- Build rapport
- Colors (get a sense of what they're thinking)
- Price / budget (surface range early)
- Agenda (tell them what to expect)
- Length of appointment (set expectations)
- Trial close

**Prep checklist:**
- Send confirmation email
- Call to confirm the day before
- Look appropriate: CertaPro Gold shirt, wrapped vehicle

---

### STAGE 2 — ARRIVAL & INTRODUCTION
**Goal:** First impression, continue rapport

- Call when en route
- Arrive on time (5 min early; call if late)
- Park on the street
- Look appropriate
- Continue building rapport (use F.O.R.M — see below)

**F.O.R.M. Rapport Framework:**
- **F** — Family
- **O** — Occupation
- **R** — Recreation
- **M** — Motivation (what's driving the project?)

---

### STAGE 3 — THE UPFRONT 4 (set the agenda at the door)
**Goal:** Frame the entire appointment before starting

Tell the customer upfront: *"While we discuss what we'll be painting, we'll talk about four things:"*
1. Color and number of coats
2. Surface preparation
3. Any minor maintenance or necessary repairs
4. The Certainty Pledge®

Then set the next step expectation:
*"After I take measurements and complete the estimate we will sit down, I'll print/email the proposal, and we can review some options."*

---

### STAGE 4 — 1ST GO AROUND (walkthrough + scope)
**Goal:** Scope the job, understand needs, begin Pledge

**Scope:**
- Includes / excludes
- Color and coats (per area)
- Surface prep and repairs

**Needs:**
- Begin asking Certainty Pledge® questions
- Uncover customer's desired price range
- "Find it, share it, sell it" — demonstrate expertise on the spot
- Discuss "The List" (customer's wish list / priorities)

*(See separate 1st Go Around Checklist for full detail)*

---

### STAGE 5 — CLIENT MANUAL
**Goal:** Build credibility, set proposal expectations

- Present the e-portfolio and CSS (Customer Satisfaction Survey)
- Direct customer to sections of special interest
- Set time, location, and expectation for proposal presentation
- Tell them what happens next

---

### STAGE 6 — 2ND GO AROUND (measurement + documentation)
**Goal:** Capture everything needed to build the estimate

- Enter information into CertaOne
- Take photos: includes, excludes, and questionable areas
- Enter notes for proposal and work order
- Re-confirm anything unclear from the 1st Go Around

---

### STAGE 7 — ROUGH ESTIMATE & PJCC
**Goal:** Build options in the field

- Create in the home or in the car
- Validate the PJCC (Project Job Cost Calculator)
- Build **2-3 options**, with at least one in the customer's desired price range
- Options allow the customer to choose — gives them control (critical for D-styles)

---

### STAGE 8 — PREPARE PROPOSAL & CERTAINTY PLEDGE®
**Goal:** Craft a tailored, customer-specific proposal

- Create a **customer experience statement** based on Certainty Pledge responses
- Specify includes and excludes for each option
- Sell benefits matched to customer's needs

---

### STAGE 9 — PRESENTATION OF PROPOSAL & THE PLEDGE
**Goal:** Walk through the proposal, confirm alignment, get yes

- Present the full proposal packet
- Present prices for each option — **PAUSE after each price** (let it land)
- Start at the top — read each word/section completely
- Get the customer to say **"Yes"** to confirm each section is accurate
- Final question: *"Are you confident this proposal and the Certainty Pledge meet your needs?"*

---

### STAGE 10 — ASK FOR THE ORDER
**Goal:** Close. Every time. No exceptions.

- Ask for the order **no matter what**
- Standard close: *"When can we get you on the schedule — the week of ___ or ___?"*
- Handle hesitations, then **ask again**

---

### +1 — FOLLOW UP
**Goal:** Keep the deal alive if not closed on the spot

- Decide: follow up in person or by phone
- Set a **specific date and time** — max 2-3 days later
- Ask for the order again

## ARIA COACHING — STAGE TRACKING

ARIA tracks which stage the rep is in based on transcript and surfaces the right coaching for that stage.

## DISC INTEGRATION BY STAGE

| Stage | D (Eagle) | I (Parrot) | S (Dove) | C (Owl) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upfront 4 | Lead with results/timeline | Make it exciting, paint the vision | Explain process for comfort | Detail all 4 items thoroughly |
| 1st Go Around | Move fast, bottom-line scope | Spend time on color/vision | Earn trust, go slow on prep | Answer all questions fully |
| Proposal | 2-3 options, lead with ROI | Lead with experience/vision | Reassure on every section | Walk through line by line |
| Ask for Order | Direct close, give them control | Close while energy is high | Gentle, give them the option | Give time, ask after they've reviewed |
| Follow Up | Phone, brief, direct | High energy call, stay excited | Warm personal touch | Send written summary first |

## ADDITIONAL COACHING NOTES

- Always sell **benefits matched to customer needs** — not features
- Use the Certainty Pledge® as a discovery tool, not just a form
- "Find it, share it, sell it" = turn every observation into a value moment
- The +1 Follow Up is not optional — if no close, always leave with a set date/time
