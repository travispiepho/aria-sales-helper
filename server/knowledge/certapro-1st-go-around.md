## WHAT IS THE 1ST GO AROUND?

The 1st Go Around is the initial walkthrough of the customer's property with the rep. It's the core scoping + trust-building conversation. ARIA listens during this phase and coaches the rep to hit every required checkpoint.

---

## CHECKLIST — WHAT MUST BE COVERED

### 1. Confirm Scope
- What areas are being painted?
- What's included vs. excluded?

### 2. Project Information
- "When do you want it painted?"
- "Why now?" — uncover the motivation behind the project
- What's driving the timeline?

### 3. Includes & Excludes
- Explicitly confirm what IS and IS NOT included
- Avoid scope creep surprises later

### 4. Color Discussion (per area)
- For each area: "Do you want to keep or change the color?"
- Offer color consultation if appropriate

### 5. Primer & Coats
- Discuss primer (per franchise guidelines)
- 1 coat or 2 coats?
- Explain why it matters

### 6. Setup & Prep
- What setup and prep IS included
- What the customer is responsible for
- What prep may incur additional cost

### 7. Carpentry & Repairs
- Discuss if applicable
- Identify issues during walkthrough, address proactively

### 8. The 4 Stages of a Paint Job
Walk the customer through all four stages — especially the importance of setup and surface prep:
1. **Set Up** — protecting surfaces, moving furniture
2. **Prep** — sanding, caulking, priming, filling holes
3. **Painting** — application of primer/paint/coats
4. **Cleanup** — protection removal, touch-ups, final walk

### 9. The Certainty Pledge®
CertaPro's signature customer-alignment tool. Purpose:
- Understand customer preferences
- Create a smoother project
- Less disruptive to customer
- Better overall experience
- Ensure the project meets their needs

**How to use it:**
- Can ask Pledge questions all at once OR break them up during the walkthrough
- Can ask during 1st Go Around OR after 2nd Go Around
- Most reps ask at least some questions during the 1st Go Around

### 10. Preferred Price Range
- Confirm what was heard during the Setup Call
- OR check if customer has a clearer idea of the range now
- Get budget alignment early — avoid sticker shock at estimate delivery

### 11. Demonstrate Expertise
- Explain technical info accurately but clearly (don't over-jargon)
- "Find it, share it, sell it" — if something comes up, use it as a selling moment
- Can contact paint store rep or production manager on the spot if needed

### 12. Discuss Options
- Ask what options the customer is open to
- Suggest options proactively (upsell opportunities: cabinet refinishing, additional rooms, deck, etc.)

### 13. Confirm Photo Permission
- Always confirm it's okay to take photos
- (Ties into CompanyCam integration — Phase 5)

## ARIA REAL-TIME COACHING — 1ST GO AROUND

ARIA tracks which checklist items have been covered based on transcript and surfaces reminders for any missed items.

### Trigger Phrases ARIA Listens For:

| Checklist Item | Trigger Phrases |
|---|---|
| Scope confirmed | "areas," "rooms," "exterior," "what are we painting" |
| Why now / motivation | "why now," "what's the occasion," "what prompted," "why are you painting" |
| Color discussion | "keep the color," "change the color," "color consultation," "what color" |
| Primer/coats | "primer," "one coat," "two coats," "how many coats" |
| Setup & prep | "setup," "prep," "surface prep," "what's included," "additional cost" |
| Carpentry | "carpentry," "repairs," "caulking," "wood rot," "trim damage" |
| 4 stages | "four stages," "stages of the job," "setup prep painting cleanup" |
| Certainty Pledge | "certainty pledge," "pledge," "preferences," "make sure we meet your needs" |
| Price range | "budget," "price range," "ballpark," "what were you thinking" |
| Options | "options," "also do," "while we're here," "have you thought about" |
| Photos | "okay to take photos," "can I photograph," "take some pictures" |

### Coaching Prompts (displayed to rep, never spoken):

**Early in walkthrough:**
- 🔲 "Ask: 'When do you want this done — and what's driving the timing?'"
- 🔲 "Confirm scope: what's IN and what's OUT"

**Mid walkthrough (per area):**
- 🔲 "Ask color: 'Keep or change?'"
- 🔲 "Primer + coats not discussed yet"

**Before wrapping up:**
- 🔲 "Haven't covered the 4 stages yet — walk them through setup/prep/paint/cleanup"
- 🔲 "Certainty Pledge not started — ask preference questions"
- 🔲 "Budget/price range not confirmed"
- 🔲 "Options not discussed — any upsell opportunities?"
- 🔲 "Confirm photo permission before leaving"

## DISC INTEGRATION — ADAPT THE GO AROUND BY STYLE

Combined with the DISC framework, ARIA coaches the rep to adjust their approach:

| Style | 1st Go Around Adaptation |
|---|---|
| **D (Eagle)** | Move fast. Hit scope, price range, and timeline first. Skip lengthy stage explanations unless they ask. |
| **I (Parrot)** | Make it exciting. Paint the vision of the finished space. Spend time on color — they love it. Don't rush. |
| **S (Dove)** | Go slow. Cover prep and process thoroughly — predictability calms them. Ask about family/home story. |
| **C (Owl)** | Be thorough. Explain all 4 stages in detail. Bring specs. Answer every question fully. Offer to follow up in writing. |
